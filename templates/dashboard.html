Formula basis

KPBI gas prices are multiplied by MRP to resolve USD/MMBtu; custom gas prices are already USD/MMBtu and use the workbook's reference-year escalation formula. Workbook NCF = Revenue - Royalty - CAPEX - OPEX - ABEX - Export Duty - PITA. Royalty is 10% of total revenue; export duty is 10% of positive liquid revenue net of liquid royalty and the entered liquid cost base. PITA is 38% after the workbook's 28% initial and 8% following-nine-year depreciation allowances, QPE carry-forward, and adjusted-loss carry-forward. The cutoff year is the first year where cumulative revenue less OPEX reaches its maximum; NCF after that year is zero. Blank liquid capex/opex/abex allocations are treated as zero.

NPV0 = NPV(0%, annual NCF) × (1 + 0%). NPV@WACC = NPV(WACC, annual NCF) × (1 + WACC)Disc_Year - start year + 1, using the workbook's 2026 Disc_Year default. Project IRR = IRR(annual NCF series). If IRR is unavailable but both NPV0 and NPV@WACC are positive, IRR is assumed to be 30%. Economic limit display = MAX(year where gated NCF ≠ 0, selected start year).

Red: IRR < 11% (not viable). Yellow: 11% ≤ IRR < 20% (viable). Green: IRR ≥ 20%, or both NPV0 and NPV@WACC are positive and IRR is assumed to be 30%.
